# Using Tangent

Tangent has three apps on one sign-in. The **Power | Learn | Canvas** switch (in the power app's sidebar, Learn's header and the canvas bar) moves between them.

- **Power** (`/`): every control. Your own API keys, or Tangent credit where it is sold.
- **Learn** (`/learn/`): a tutor with nothing to configure.
- **Canvas** (`/canvas/`): experimental. Your conversations as lanes on one surface.

Power, Learn and Canvas share one account: a conversation started in one is listed and can be continued in the others. They are views of the same conversations, so a conversation can be opened in any of them: with one open, the switch opens it, on the same thread, in the other app. Learn replies on its one model, Normal: a thread on another provider or model, power's Max included, is answered by Normal there (or the open pool's model), and keeps its own model in power.

Tangent is in beta: a notice at the top of each app asks you to report problems to `admin@` the site's domain. Closing it hides it for a day in that browser.

On an Android phone, Learn and power offer the Android app, Learn made for a phone on the same account, in a notice at the top from your second visit in that browser (the landing page links it too). It shows in place of the beta notice. **Get it on Google Play** opens its store page; following it or closing the notice hides it in that browser for good.

Signing in by email sends one email with a link and a 6-digit code. The link signs you in wherever you open it. Every app also takes the code: type or paste it on the **Check your email** screen, which helps when the email is on another device. Google and GitHub sign you in too, creating your account the first time, as the email does. The phone apps sign in by code, and the Android app also with Google, from the phone's own account picker; the link in the email signs in only a browser. If the app is closed while you read the email, Learn reopens on **Check your email** for that address until the code expires, so you don't need another email. Each works once and expires in 15 minutes; after three wrong codes, ask for a new email. In the web apps, opening a link to a conversation (or any page that needs you signed in) while signed out takes you to sign in and then back to that page, whichever way you sign in, an email link opened in another browser included.

When Tangent is full (a deployment can cap its number of users), the login page still signs existing users in, and offers new people **Join the waitlist**: you get one email when there's room.

## Reply or tangent

Every conversation has a **main thread**, and any message can start a **tangent**: a thread of its own that starts from a message and never writes back into the thread it came from. In Learn a tangent sees everything before it; in power its context mode decides. A tangent can have tangents of its own. (The code and the API call them branches.)

**Tangents are how you explore. Next is a plain reply.** "Wait, tell me more about that" or "Hey, what about…?" is a tangent: it goes deeper into something the reply said, or off to something related, in a thread of its own, and the lesson stays as it was. **Next ➜** is good old-fashioned AI chat, adding your message to the thread you're in, there if you want it.

**Not sure? Tangent.** A needless tangent costs nothing: you come back and carry on. A stray reply stays in the main thread, the model reads it on every later turn, and the lesson no longer reads straight through. Tangents are footnotes that can grow into books of their own. Inside a tangent, going on in it is the natural step, so there **Next ➜** is the default.

- **How tangents work** says this in short: the **?** in Learn's header, **How tangents work** at the foot of the power app's sidebar, the **?** in the Flutter and phone apps' bars, and a card on the landing page (`/welcome#how-tangents`). In the web apps the help links to the keyboard shortcuts, and the `?` key still opens those.
- **The first-run walkthrough:** in Learn (web, Flutter and the phone apps) a new account is walked round the loop once, in three short notes: after the first reply, one at **Tangent ↳**; inside the tangent, one at the way back to the **Main thread**; back on the main thread, one at **Next ➜**, and replying with it (or **Done**) finishes the tour. They never block the page: you move on by using the control they point at. **Skip tour** (or `Esc`) ends it at any step. Finished or skipped, it doesn't come back on any device; **Replay the walkthrough** in the help runs it again. The demos show it on every new visit.

## How Tangent answers

Both apps answer with the same built-in system prompt (in power, unless you set your own): it answers the question asked, directly and in depth, and never quizzes you. Whatever you don't follow, you go on a tangent into. Every substantive reply ends with suggested tangents in two groups under it. Clicking any of them opens a tangent titled after it and asks it, so you can follow one, come back, and try another:

- **Dig deeper**: the questions you might ask next about what the reply explained, going further into it.
- **Suggested tangents**: related topics further out, worth exploring on the side.

Above them, **Your tangents** lists the tangents you started from that reply, from a suggestion, the message box or a question of your own; clicking one opens it where it starts. The one on the thread you're in, and the one you just came back from, are highlighted. Once a reply has a tangent of yours, its suggestions fold behind one row (**4 suggested tangents**, or **3 more suggested** once you followed one); click it to show them again. Shares and exports list the suggestions as **Dig deeper** and **Tangents**.

While a reply has no tangent of yours, the Flutter and phone apps show its first suggestion with **N more suggested** for the rest. Inside a tangent, its path shows the **Main thread**, the tangent it came from, and **…** for any steps between. The outline (the **Tangents** button on a phone, beside the thread on a wide screen) lists every tangent in the lesson, nested under the one each came from. On a phone the header slides away while you read down and comes back when you scroll up.

In power, Learn and the Flutter and phone apps, opening a tangent shows it from its start, where it leaves the message it came from, every time you open it; the main thread opens at its end. While the end of a long thread is out of sight, a down-arrow button above the message box jumps to it. Clicking or tapping the title, or the open thread's own name in the path, goes back to its start, as does tapping the status bar on an iPhone. On a keyboard, `Home` and `End` do the same while you aren't typing.

**Diagrams.** When a picture would make an answer clearer, the tutor offers a specific one in its last sentence (say, a sequence diagram of a handshake); ask, and it draws it in the reply once the reply has finished. On the web, **Show source** shows the text behind it. A diagram that can't be drawn shows that text, with a note; share links and exports always show the text.

**Checking facts.** On Tangent credit, and on OpenRouter or Anthropic with your own key, a reply can search the web when it likely needs to: deep in a tangent, a specific fact, something recent, or when you ask for sources. A grounded reply cites its claims as links and lists its sources ("Checked against 3 sources"). An unchecked one says "From the tutor's own knowledge", with a **Check sources** button that searches and adds a corrected, cited answer. The open pool never searches.

## The power app

- **Replying:** the message box works as in [Learn](#learn). After a reply, **Tangent ↳** sends your message as a new tangent from the reply you're on and **Next ➜** as the next message of the thread you're in; `Enter` sends with the filled one (**Tangent ↳** on the main thread, **Next ➜** inside a tangent) and `Ctrl`/`Cmd+Enter` with the other. On a phone or tablet `Enter` starts a new line and only the buttons send. **Compare** sits beside them. The caret on **Tangent ↳** (or `Ctrl`/`Cmd+Shift+Enter`) opens **Tangent from here** from the same reply, with the words on the box as its quote and what you typed as its starting message; the box empties once that message is asked, edited there or not, and **Cancel** leaves it as it was. A question of your own about an earlier reply starts from that reply's **Tangent ↳**, below.
- **Ask about this:** select text in a finished message and **Ask about this** appears above the message box. It puts the words on the message box ("Ask about this…"), focused: your next message asks about them in a new tangent that quotes them (**Compare** waits until then), and **×** takes them off. On a thread the membership locks, which has no message box, it reads **Tangent from this selection…** and opens **Tangent from here** with the quote. The caret on the box's **Tangent ↳** then opens **Tangent from here** with the quote filled in. Selecting and pressing `b` puts the words on the message box too; `Shift+B` opens the dialog with them.
- **Tangent ↳**, on any message, puts that message on the message box ("Tangent from “…”"), or the words you highlighted in it, and focuses the box: your next message asks about it in a new tangent, and **×** takes it off. `b` does the same for the focused (or latest) reply. On a thread the membership locks, which has no message box, it opens **Tangent from here** instead.
- **Tangent from here** (`Shift+B` on the selected words or the focused message, or the caret on the message box's **Tangent ↳**) sets a tangent up before it starts: write the starting message (`Ctrl`/`Cmd+Enter` in any field creates and asks), keep or remove the quote of the words you highlighted, pick a context mode, and **Private**. Who pays, the model and web search fold into one line, and **Change** opens them. They start on the route the bar under the message box shows, or on a new conversation's default where that route can't reply in this browser, with web search as in the message's thread. A tangent starts with its first message, so **Create and ask** waits for one, and cancelling makes nothing. There is no title field: a tangent is named after its first reply; rename it in **Tangent settings** or in the outline.
- **Context modes** decide what the model sees in a tangent:
  - `path`: everything its parent saw, plus the tangent's own messages;
  - `summary`: a summary of the parent's conversation;
  - `message`: only the message the tangent starts from, plus the quote;
  - `independent`: only the quote or topic.
- **Your tangents** under a message lists the tangents started from it, each with its context mode and message count. Breadcrumbs and **↩ Parent message** go back to the exact message a tangent started from.
- **Deleting a tangent**, with everything below it, after a confirmation: the trash beside it under a message's **Your tangents**, beside **↩ Parent message**, in the outline or in **Tangent settings**. Conversations delete from the home page and the sidebar.
- **Deleting a message**: the trash on one of your messages deletes it with everything after it in its thread, the later messages and the tangents started from them, after a confirmation that counts them. Deleting a tangent's first message deletes the tangent. There is no editing a message: delete it and ask again.
- **Context Inspector** (`i`): exactly what the next message will send, and why: inherited messages, summaries, the quote, and what was compacted or dropped.
- **Review up to here** (on any reply, or `v`): sends the conversation, as the model saw it, to a reviewer model: your default reviewer, else the default model of the thread's provider (of another provider you can use when that one has no key here or needs a membership). Ticking **Use as my default reviewer**, under the dialog's model picker, makes the picked model your default at once (saved in this browser); it shows ticked only while the picked model is the default, and unticking it goes back to the thread's provider's. The review lists corrections and says whether to continue on a stronger model; one click moves the thread to the reviewer's model, starts a tangent on it (**Tangent from here** on the reviewer's model, for your question), or puts the corrections in the message box. Reviews aren't stored.
- **Normal | Max and Compare:** a switch in the bar under the message box puts the thread on your Normal or Max (the suggested models, unless you chose others in **Settings**, which **Edit Normal and Max…** beside the switch opens there), and the bar names the tier the thread is on. **Tangent settings** and **Conversation settings** offer the same two first among the model chips, each on the payer you chose it with. **Compare** asks both and lets you keep one answer. Only the answer you keep enters the conversation, and both are paid for. A new conversation starts on your Normal when you chose one in **Settings** and it can reply in this browser (on Tangent credit, while you have some left). Otherwise it starts on a provider you have a key for, else on Tangent credit while you have some, else on OpenRouter with your own key, which the first send asks for; while your own keys need a membership you don't have, Tangent credit comes first. The home page's picker shows which, and who pays, before you send.
- **The bar under the message box** shows the thread's route (provider, who pays, model); clicking it opens where to change it: **Tangent settings** in a tangent, and on the main thread **Conversation settings**, at the main thread's replies. Every new tangent starts on that route, even one from a message further up the path, so the bar says who pays for it. If the thread is on your own key and this browser has no key for it, it says so before you send, with **Use Tangent credit** and **Add your key**.
- **Links between messages:** **Link…** on any message (or `l`) links it to another message of the conversation, with an optional note: search or browse, or **Pick on the page instead**. Linked messages show **N related** chips at both ends; a chip opens the other end, with a **Back to ‘…’** pill. Links aren't sent to the model.
- **Text size:** **Aa** in the chat header (85% to 140%), or `-`, `+` and `0` outside a text field. It applies to the messages and the message box, and is saved in this browser; Learn and Canvas have their own.
- **Settings** (sidebar): the defaults for every conversation in power, grouped by what they control, each saying where it is saved.
  - **Normal and Max** (saved in this browser): each is **Suggested**, the model the thread's payer suggests for that tier, or a model you choose, which runs on the payer you chose with it.
  - **How replies are written:** your default system prompt in power and Canvas, used by every conversation without a prompt of its own, including ones you started before you changed it (synced to your account; Learn's lessons keep the tutor prompt; **Start from the built-in prompt** copies the built-in one into the box, and an empty box means the built-in one). Then the **Reply limit** (saved in this browser), a hard stop: a reply that reaches it is cut off.
  - **What each message sends** (saved in this browser): the **Input limit** caps how much of a conversation each message sends (16,000 to 128,000 tokens, or a custom number); over it, the oldest messages are summarized (the default) or dropped. As you edit it, it shows what that means in words, pages and, where the price is known, dollars.
- **Conversation settings** (**Conversation** beside the title): the conversation's title and own system prompt, how the main thread replies (who pays, the model and web search, which new tangents from the main thread start with), and **Private** for the whole conversation. Leave the prompt empty, or clear it, and the conversation follows your default system prompt, whatever it is when you send. The main thread is always called that, and can't be renamed.
- **Tangent settings** (**Tangent** beside **↩ Parent message**, inside a tangent): its title, context mode and quote, how it replies (who pays, the model and web search, which new tangents from it start with), and **Private**.
- **Unsaved edits:** **Save** and **Cancel** stay in view at the foot of a long dialog. In **Settings**, **Conversation settings**, **Tangent settings** and **Tangent from here**, `Escape` or a tap outside the dialog asks before discarding your edits, with **Keep editing** the default; **Cancel** and the **×** discard them.
- **Private tangents** are left out of every share and export, with everything below them; a private main thread leaves out the whole conversation.
- **Sharing:** **Share…** in the chat header picks a scope (the whole conversation, a tangent and everything below it, or one path), with an optional title and expiry. A link shows the conversation as it was when shared; later messages reach it only when you republish it. The dialog first lists the links the conversation already has. The **Shares** page lists every link; republish a link in place or revoke it, which takes effect at once. A shared page is kept out of search engines, labels messages "User" and "AI", and tells readers the replies are AI-generated. Until the operator turns sharing on for everyone, **Share…** appears only for allowed accounts. A new account can publish links after its first day, and each account can create 20 links a day; deleting a link doesn't give it back, and a publish the screening refuses uses one up too. What a link publishes (a new link, a republish or a new title) is screened automatically first; a conversation the screening flags can't be shared, and the app says only that. Every shared page has a **Report this conversation** link, for anyone who opens it, signed in or not; a report may take the link down while it is reviewed. A link taken down shows as **unavailable** on the Shares page, and the only thing left to do with it is delete it.
- **Export:** Markdown, or one offline HTML file with the same viewer as share links.
- **Backup:** **Export → JSON backup (everything)** includes everything, private tangents and links too. **Import** restores it as a new conversation. Files over 10 MB, Markdown or HTML exports and other JSON are refused with a message saying why.
- **Keys & credit** (sidebar): your own provider keys, and where credit is sold the balance and **Add credit**. **Billing** (`/billing`): the membership, balance, top-ups and recent usage. To add credit, pick an amount or type your own: the page shows the credit, the service fee on it and the subtotal before tax, and **Add credit** opens the secure checkout, where tax is added. You get the full amount of credit, and each reply takes its model's list price from it.

### Without a membership

Where the operator charges a membership, replies on your own keys need one. Without it, nothing is locked away: conversations stay listed, readable, exportable and manageable. A thread on your own key shows a notice where the message box was: **Renew membership** (or **Become a member**), **Open in Learn** (the same conversation in Learn, where the open pool or Tangent credit can reply), and, while credit can pay, **Continue with Tangent credit**, which moves that thread onto credit. Its replies' suggested tangents fold behind one row, since none can start there; your tangents under them still open.

## Learn

Learn keeps only the essentials:

- a list of lessons, with **Import** above it and **Export** (the download icon) on each lesson;
- a chat on one model, Normal, with no model to pick (on your own Anthropic key, Claude Sonnet, below);
- the tutor's **Dig deeper** and **Suggested tangents** under each reply, below **Your tangents** ([How Tangent answers](#how-tangent-answers));
- two buttons under the message box after a reply, always in this order: **Tangent ↳** ("Wait, tell me more about that") sends your message as a new tangent from that reply, and **Next ➜** (plain chat) sends it as the next message of the thread you're in, the main thread or, inside a tangent, that tangent. Their tooltips (a long press in the Flutter and phone apps) say what each does. `Enter` sends with the filled button and `Ctrl`/`Cmd+Enter` with the other (`Shift+Enter` for a new line): on the main thread **Tangent ↳** is filled, inside a tangent **Next ➜**, so pressing `Enter` without thinking never goes more than one tangent deep. On a phone or tablet `Enter` starts a new line instead, and only a tap on a button sends. With nothing to go on a tangent from (a new lesson, a reply that failed) there is just **Send**. The box grows to three lines while you type in it. The Flutter and phone apps work the same way;
- the **Tangent ↳** under the message box starts from the reply you are on, else the last reply of the thread you're in. On the web that is one you clicked or reached with `j` / `k` in that thread (a question stands for its reply, so in a tangent you opened at its start, its first reply); in the Flutter and phone apps, a tangent's first reply, from when you open the tangent until you send or jump to the end. The messages of earlier threads above it are its context: to go on a tangent from one, use **Ask about this** or its own **Tangent ↳** (on the web, also `b`). When the reply isn't the last one, or the end is out of view, the message box names it once you click or tap into it ("↳ from “…”"); click or tap that to see the reply. Jumping to the end (`End` or the down arrow) lets a marked reply go;
- **Ask about this** on selected text (above the message box on the web, in the selection menu in the Flutter and phone apps) puts the words on the message box ("Ask about this…"). Your next message asks about them in a new tangent, which shows the quote under its title, so only **Tangent ↳** shows; **×** takes them off. The **Tangent ↳** in a reply's header puts that whole reply on the box the same way;
- **Connect** on a message: link it to a related message of the lesson, with an optional note; both then list it under **Connected to N**;
- finding your way: a tangent opens at its first message. Inside one, the path (**Main thread** › … › here) returns to the message it started from; **Main thread** goes back to the lesson's main thread. On a phone the path shows its start and its last steps. The **sidebar** (the menu button on a phone) lists your lessons, and under the open one the main thread and every tangent in it, nested under the one each came from; delete a lesson or tangent from its trash. Click a message to mark it (the URL then points at it). On a keyboard: `Alt`+arrows or `[` / `]` between tangents, `j` / `k` between messages, `Home` / `End` to the start or end of the thread, `b` puts the marked (or last) reply on the message box, `/` the message box, `?` lists them all;
- **How tangents work** (the **?** in the header) and the first-run walkthrough ([Reply or tangent](#reply-or-tangent));
- deleting a tangent with everything below it (the trash beside it under **Your tangents**, or beside it in the sidebar);
- deleting one of your messages with everything after it in its thread, the later messages and the tangents started from them (the trash beside **You**, in the Flutter and phone apps too); deleting a tangent's first message deletes the tangent;
- **Aa** for the lesson's text size;
- **Your instructions** (the pencil in a lesson's header, while replies are paid with your own key or credit): instructions of your own for that lesson, such as "Answer in French", added after the tutor's. The open pool ignores them, and so does power;
- **How replies are paid for** (account menu, or the **Paid by** pill): your own API key, **Tangent credit** (prepaid; the header shows the balance), or the **open pool** (free within daily limits, while it has credit). Your own key is an OpenRouter key or, where the server offers it, an Anthropic key (an Anthropic plan that includes API credit pays for it); pick which under **Which key**. On an Anthropic key, replies use Claude Sonnet and titles and summaries Claude Haiku, with nothing to configure: power mode is where to pick other models. A key saved in either app works in both. Where the membership is required, your own key needs it; credit and the pool never do. While both credit and the pool can pay, a switch above the message box picks between them, with a third stop naming your key (**OpenRouter key** or **Anthropic key**, as the **Paid by** pill does) once a key you can use is saved. A message refused for want of a key, credit or membership is kept and sent once you pick a way on. The Flutter and phone apps have the same dialog in their account menu, and a refused message stays in the message box with **How you pay**, to send again once you pick; they keep the key encrypted (on a phone, in its keychain) and send members to the web for the membership and credit;
- **Billing** (`/learn/billing`): the membership, and with credit the balance, top-ups (as in power) and recent usage.
- **Delete account** (account menu, here and in the Flutter and phone apps, or `/learn/delete-account` as a page of its own): deletes the account all three apps share, once you retype your email. The public `/delete-account` page says what is deleted and kept, and its button signs you in and opens that page.

An import into Learn is adapted so it can be continued there: threads on a provider or model Learn doesn't offer move to Normal, and every tangent gets the whole path as context. Learn answers with its tutor prompt whatever prompt the conversation had in power mode. Nothing is charged by an import.

## Canvas (experimental)

A view of the same conversations: anything started in power or Learn opens on the canvas and the other way round.

- **Every thread is a lane** on one pannable, zoomable surface, a tangent's lane hanging to the right of the message it starts from. The curve's stroke shows its context mode (solid `path`, dashed `summary`, dash-dot `message`, dotted `independent`).
- **Every lane has its own message box and streams on its own;** the bar counts how many are writing.
- **Tangent from here, in variants:** the tangent button opens one lane or several at once, each with its own mode and model, and the starting message they all start with ("Every context mode" asks the same question three ways).
- **Dig deeper**, **Suggested tangents** and **Ask about this** work as in power, and **Ask your own question…** ends each finished reply's suggestions, in view when they fold (open on the selected lane's newest card): each opens a new lane. In **Ask your own**, Enter asks (Shift+Enter for a new line; on a phone or tablet `Enter` starts one too, and only **Ask** asks; Escape folds it), and the caret joined to **Ask** opens **Tangent from here** with the question. **Ask about this** opens a box over the canvas for your question, with the quote; the lane opens once you ask it, and Escape or **×** drops it. The lanes started from a card show on it as chips, under **Your tangents**.
- **Lineage:** with a lane selected, the cards the model would see light up; summarized and dropped ones are marked.
- **Fold** a lane's subtree into a capsule; a minimap and keyboard navigation (`?` lists it) cover the rest.
- **Links** across lanes: drag the port on a card's edge onto another card, or press `r` for pick mode.
- **Delete** a lane with every lane below it from the trash in its head.
- **Delete** one of your messages, with everything after it in its lane and the lanes started from those, from the trash on its card.
- **Aa** in the bar sets the cards' text size (`+`, `-` and `0` zoom the canvas).

There is no reviewer, share, export or settings editor on the canvas; use the power app for those.

## The demos

`/learn/demo`, `/demo` and `/canvas/demo` run the apps entirely in the browser: no sign-in, no model calls, and nothing kept after the tab closes. Replies are random English sentences, but tangents, suggestions, "Ask about this", Check sources (on pretend sources), export and import behave as in the real app.
